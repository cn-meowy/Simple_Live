MeowLive Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

The MeowLive team understands the importance of personal information to you and will do its best to protect your privacy. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agreed to all of the contents of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection

  1. The App does not collect, store, or use any of your personal information, including but not limited to your name, address, email address, phone number, or national ID number.
  2. The App does not perform any form of personal-information collection during use.
  3. If you voluntarily provide personal information while using the App, you will be deemed to have provided it of your own accord and will bear all resulting legal responsibility.

2. Local Storage

  1. Some App settings and your usage data (such as the server address, follow list and watch history) are stored only locally on your device, using the local-storage mechanisms provided by the operating system.
  2. These local data are not automatically uploaded to any server, unless you actively enable device sync and configure a sync server.
  3. When you uninstall the App, the local data described above are removed together with the App.

3. Device Sync (Optional)

  1. The App offers an optional device-sync feature that synchronises your follow list and watch history across your devices.
  2. This feature requires you to configure a sync-server address yourself. Synced data is transmitted to the server you designate; the developers do not access or store such data.
  3. Please ensure the sync server you configure is safe and reliable. The developers are not responsible for any data leakage or loss caused by issues with that server.

4. Network Access

  1. The App requires network access to retrieve live-stream lists, playback URLs and danmaku. These requests are sent directly to the server you configure and to the relevant third-party live-streaming platforms.
  2. The App does not transmit your usage data to any statistics or analytics service.

5. Third-Party Services

  1. All live-streaming services, media nodes, stream sources and stream URLs used by the App are built, configured and integrated with third-party servers by the user. When you access content on those platforms, you are subject to their respective privacy policies.
  2. The App is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms; please refer to each platform's privacy policy.

6. Protection of Minors

  1. The App is not intended to provide services specifically for minors and does not actively collect personal information from minors.
  2. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a guardian, who shall bear the corresponding supervisory responsibility.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  1. The developers reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time without further notice.
  2. After the policy is changed, your continued use of the App shall be deemed acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developers at meowycn@163.com.

If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App. Use of the App indicates that you have fully understood and agreed to the contents above.
